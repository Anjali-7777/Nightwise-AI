Core GenAI Concepts (Syllabus Guide)

1. Scaled Dot-Product Attention:

Computes similarity between the Query vector of a current word and Key vectors of all prior words, scaled by \( \sqrt{d_k} \) to avoid exploding gradients in large dimensions.

2. Multi-Head Attention:

Splits hidden vectors into multiple heads so the model can simultaneously focus on syntax (verbs), entities (places), and timing constraints ("2 AM").

3. Why Attention Heatmaps Matter:

Reveals how the model extracts intent: when parsing "walking home at 2 AM", high attention weights flow from "safest" to "walking" and "2 AM", configuring night-time weights rather than daytime driving heuristics.
